Research Focus

My research work focuses on applying data science, statistical modelling, spatial analysis, and mathematical methods to solve real-world problems. I am especially interested in using data-driven approaches to support public health decision-making and community development.

Spatial Analysis of Child Malnutrition in Rwanda

District-Level Integration of DHS Data and Agro-Climatic Indicators

This current research investigates spatial patterns of child malnutrition in Rwanda by integrating Rwanda DHS data with district-level agro-climatic indicators such as rainfall, vegetation conditions, and administrative boundary data.

The study applies spatial statistical methods to identify geographic clustering, district-level variation, and possible environmental factors associated with child malnutrition outcomes.

Application of Frobenius Method for Solving Ordinary Differential Equations

Ordinary Differential Equations Involving Variable Coefficients

This undergraduate research focused on the application of the Frobenius method for solving ordinary differential equations involving variable coefficients, especially equations whose solutions cannot be easily obtained using elementary methods.

The work demonstrated how power series solutions can be constructed around regular singular points and how the method can be applied systematically to obtain meaningful mathematical solutions.
